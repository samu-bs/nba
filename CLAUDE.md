### Player-access strategy — REVISED 2026-08-16 after research
**The old assumption was wrong.** All on-court kid experiences are GROUP-SALES ONLY:
Nets (High Five Kids Tunnel, Anthem Buddies, Captains Huddle, Pregame Shootaround) require
**10+**; Knicks/MSG ("high-fiving the team as they enter the court") requires **9+**.
A party of 3 does not qualify. Also: **no reputable vendor sells a guaranteed meet & greet
with a current Knicks/Nets player for a regular-season game.** Quint (official NBA partner)
covers only All-Star/Draft/Global Games. "VIP packages" = good seats + a signed ball, not a meeting.

What actually works, in priority order:
1. **★ Barclays Pre-Game Pass — $75 pp, sold to INDIVIDUALS on Ticketmaster.** Entry 1.5h before
   regular doors (Barclays doors = 90 min pre-tip, so ~3h early), behind-the-scenes, and
   **watch both teams warm up from prime sideline seats steps from the floor.** Requires a
   separate game ticket; kids 2+ need their own; no refunds. **NOT ON SALE YET** — Barclays tour
   tickets release 4–6 weeks ahead → expect ~mid-Nov 2026 for the 21.12 game. SET A REMINDER.
   Also sold as a separate Ticketmaster event listing "Nets v. X: Pre-Game Pass (Watch Warm-Ups)".
2. **MSG Tour** $48 adult / $43 child — Knicks locker room open **only on NON-game days** →
   book for 22.12 or 24.12.
3. **shop.msg.com Knicks-alumni meet & greet charity auctions** (Garden of Dreams): 2× 100-level
   tickets + pregame warmup access + escorted meeting with a Knicks alumnus. Real and recurring;
   collection empty in offseason. **Check weekly from Oct 2026.**
4. DIY: Barclays policy = autographs **only from your ticketed seat**, no designated areas.
   Player tunnels at Barclays are by **sections 3 and 16**; MSG **105–106** behind Knicks bench
   (verify on a seat map — sources conflict). Shootarounds closed to public at both arenas.
5. Open question worth one phone call: **groups@brooklynnets.com / 844-255-9647** — ask whether
   3 people BUYING 10 tickets (~$500 at 21.12 prices) unlocks the High Five Kids Tunnel, or
   whether 10 physical attendees are required. If it works, it's the best value on the trip.
