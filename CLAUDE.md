# CLAUDE.md — NBA Bar Mitzvah Trip

## Project goal
Plan a Bar Mitzvah celebration trip to the USA centered on attending NBA games,
inspired by the packages offered by NBATrips (https://nbatrips.com/tripshe/).
The user wants a more private/custom version rather than (or in addition to)
a large group tour.

## Travelers
- Yossi (father) + 2 sons, ages 13 and 15.
- The 13-year-old's Bar Mitzvah is the occasion.
- Possibly +1 additional parent and +1 child (so 3 confirmed, up to 5 total).
- Origin: Israel (flights from TLV; ESTA needed — biometric passports).

## Stated preferences
- **Region:** East Coast — New York and surroundings preferred.
  - Relevant East-Coast / nearby NBA teams & arenas:
    - New York Knicks — Madison Square Garden, Manhattan
    - Brooklyn Nets — Barclays Center, Brooklyn
    - Philadelphia 76ers — Wells Fargo Center, Philadelphia (~2h from NYC)
    - Boston Celtics — TD Garden, Boston (the famous "parquet" floor)
    - Washington Wizards — Capital One Arena, DC
    - Toronto Raptors — Scotiabank Arena (international, requires Canada entry)
- **Experience:** "משחקים על הפרקט" — courtside / floor-level experience,
  with the ability to **meet the players** (meet & greet / tunnel club /
  hi-five / warmup access / premium courtside packages).
- **Season:** the upcoming 2026-27 NBA season.

## Key constraints & facts
- **Schedule:** The official 2026-27 NBA schedule is released ~mid-August 2026
  (est. Aug 14–21). Exact game dates/matchups are NOT yet known as of June 2026.
  NBATrips itself states full trip details update only after the schedule drops.
- **Likely Israeli-family travel windows:** Hanukkah (Dec 2026), winter school
  break, All-Star weekend (mid-Feb 2027), Pesach (spring 2027).
- **Meet-players reality:** Official courtside seats grant proximity but not
  guaranteed player meetings. True meet & greets usually come via team premium
  experiences (e.g., tunnel/hi-five clubs), VIP hospitality vendors, or package
  tour operators — these are limited, pricey, and team-dependent.

## CONFIRMED DECISIONS (as of 2026-06-27)
- Window: **Hanukkah, Dec 4–12, 2026** (NOT All-Star — that's in Phoenix/West, no regular games).
- Travelers: **3** (Yossi + 2 sons). +parent/+child still TBD.
- Budget: **$3,000–5,000 per person** (~$9–15K total).
- Approach: **DIY custom** (not NBATrips group tour).
- Team priority: **New York Knicks**.
- Meeting players: **MUST — it's the heart of the trip.**
- Seats: **At least ONE courtside (floor) game CONFIRMED** — plan: Nets-vs-Knicks
  courtside at Barclays (~$900–1,400 pp) + lower-bowl for other games. Courtside
  doubles as best player-proximity. MSG Knicks courtside = upgrade option only.
- Plan doc: `תוכנית-טיול-בר-מצווה-NBA.md` in this folder.

### Player-access strategy (key insight)
Guaranteed personal meet&greet with a Knicks star is rare/expensive ($1–5K pp via
vendors). Realistic budget approach = layer: (a) bookable on-court kid experiences
[Nets Barclays high-five/warmup pass — esp. a Nets-vs-Knicks game; Celtics High-Five
Tunnel in Boston], (b) DIY autograph hunting at team hotel/shootaround/arena, (c) optional
paid meet&greet as a splurge.
